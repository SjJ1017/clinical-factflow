Counting & connection rules

Atoms and mentions. An atom is a distinct exact text + qualifier set within this case. Repeated appearances of that atom are mentions. Atom counts precede semantic grouping. Pair totals are n(n−1)/2, without self-pairs. Initial metadata shared by A/B/C is counted once per trace.

Saved decisions. Both directional margins ≥ threshold: equivalent. Exactly one passes: one-way entailment. Neither passes: unrelated. Blocker-rejected pairs are also unrelated. Missing scores are not treated as unrelated; all 24 ledgers passed completeness checks.

Sensitivity. Only the NLI margin threshold changes. The case-pooled blocker stays at 0.62 / top 12. All scores come from the completed Qwen3-14B run. Saved margins near this cutoff occur in 0.25 steps; thresholds 5.28 and 5.50 yield identical labels. 5.28 is inherited calibration; sensitivity is not a measurement of clinical matching accuracy.

Connections. Click an atom to highlight its exact repetitions and direct equivalent neighbors. An arrow requires equivalence between its actual endpoint atoms and a saved delivery into the receiving turn. It shows a transmission opportunity, not proof of use. Parallel outputs have no arrows. No transitive equivalence is inferred.

Provenance. Original evidence and output text are unchanged. Highlights use saved occurrence offsets. Unlocated mentions remain selectable. One quote can anchor several atoms. Domains, modality and other labels describe individual extracted mentions.

Scope. Matching pools all five conditions within a case. Trace counts and connections use only atoms present in the selected trace. Reference diagnosis is shown only for review; it was withheld from agents. Diagnosis correctness has not been adjudicated.
